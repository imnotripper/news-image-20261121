# The World in One Image — Saturday, 21 November 2026

An entry for the [make.fun bounty "Sum up 21 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121): a vintage newsprint front page that sums up Saturday 21 November 2026 in five numbered stories around a world map.

## The five stories

1. **Missiles cross into Poland** — Russian missiles cross the Polish border, triggering a red alert on NATO's eastern frontier.
2. **ICJ rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya** — the International Court of Justice in The Hague finds Myanmar guilty of genocide.
3. **Global AI shares sell off** — AI stocks plunge worldwide as the chips sector slides.
4. **UK–EU deal** — the UK and the EU sign a new cooperation pact, a handshake over the Star Bridge.
5. **Magnitude 6.8 earthquake in Peru** — a powerful quake strikes Peru's Andean region, cracking the earth.

A headline strip along the bottom repeats all five stories so the day reads at a glance.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the bundled `entry.png`
(1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (or `/output/entry.png`
in the grader). No network, fully deterministic — both runs write identical bytes.
